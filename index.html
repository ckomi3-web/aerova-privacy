AEROVA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Short version: Aerova has no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We (the developer) never receive, collect or share any of your data.

What Aerova stores

To work, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • Your body weight and chosen activity level, used to work out your daily water target
  • The water and other drinks you log
  • Your breathwork sessions (breath hold times) and cold exposure sessions
  • Badges you earn, notes you write, and your settings (reminders, units, theme)

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Aerova does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising and no advertising ID
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health-sensor access
  • No internet access is used by the app

Backups you control

  • Android backup: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Aerova's data in your own Google account backup, so it is restored when you set up a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your Google account; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.
  • Backup file: you can export a backup file and save it wherever you choose, and restore it later. The file only goes where you send it.
  • Sharing: if you share your weekly report, it is sent only to the app or person you pick.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for water reminders and to show a running timer or breathing session.
  • Run at startup: to put your water reminders back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration and keep awake: for breathing and timer cues, and to keep timers accurate with the screen off.
  • Foreground service: to keep a breathing session or timer running while the phone is locked.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Your data → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Health information

Aerova is a general wellbeing app and is not a medical device. It does not give medical advice. Breathwork with breath holds can cause light-headedness or fainting; always practise sitting or lying down, never in or near water or while driving.

Children

Aerova is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
